Who we are

Our Mission & Objectives

Annual Get Together

A signature three-day summit every August to unite members from across the globe in a celebration of heritage and friendship.

Bilateral Cooperation

Fostering mutual understanding in business and education while ensuring the diaspora integrates successfully within Hungarian society.

Institutional Liaison

Addressing association needs by engaging directly with local authorities and institutions.

Diplomatic Harmony

Maintaining and deepening the cordial relationship between Ghana and Hungary, similar to official government cooperation.

Educational Support

Assisting students with cultural adaptation, language barriers, and adherence to host country regulations.

Welfare & Solidarity

Supporting members through life’s major milestones, including weddings, funerals, and times of hardship.

Cultural Promotion

Celebrating Ghanaian identity through music, drumming, dance, fashion, and gourmet traditional dishes.
